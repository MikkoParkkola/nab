agents, or deployments. Buying a license for `nab` does not license the other

## Free use and optional support

The MIT-licensed core remains free for commercial and noncommercial use under
MIT. For Enterprise Edition code, noncommercial use and the nonprofit,
educational, public-research, government, and other public-benefit uses
permitted by PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 remain free. The licensor additionally
grants non-profit organizations permission to use Enterprise Edition code at
no charge: use by a non-profit organization is a permitted purpose under
PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0, subject to that license's other conditions. This
additional permission does not narrow any rights that license already grants.
Free users have the same published software features. No sponsorship is
required for those permitted uses.

The Coffee (USD 5 one-time), Supporter (USD 5/month), and Sustainer
(USD 25/month) tiers are optional contributions to maintenance. They do not
purchase a commercial license, support, an SLA, or custom work. Custom payments
receive the benefits of the tier GitHub assigns. A custom monthly payment
assigned to a commercial tier is subject to that tier's eligibility,
one-organization/one-product scope, and registration requirements. Paying more
does not license additional products. One-time payments and payments with no
assigned commercial tier are optional support and do not grant commercial
rights. Choose a commercial-license tier when commercial permission is required.
